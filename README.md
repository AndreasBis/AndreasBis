### Hi, I'm Andreas Bisiadis

I'm a CS student at the University of West Attica in Athens. I started doing ML on Kaggle at 16. These days most of my time goes into LLMs (both proprietary and open-weight ones), mainly how they reason and how to serve them efficiently.

#### How I got here

Kaggle taught me to ship things that work under hard limits: fixed GPUs, fixed runtimes, no second chances. That's where I found I care less about chasing leaderboards than about the systems underneath. In AIMO3 I spent most of my time tuning vLLM until GPT-OSS-120B with tool use fit the competition's budget. The [notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/andreasbis/aimo-3-gpt-oss-120b-with-tools) that came out of it was adopted by the 2nd and 7th place teams (from the private leaderboard). That work also got me invited to the [AIMO Proof Pilot](https://aimoprize.com/updates/2026-06-24-aimo-3-winners-announced), a private follow-up for 7 AIMO3 participants on proofs generated by fully open-source LLMs.

After that I wanted to go one level deeper, from configuring inference engines to changing them.

#### What I'm working on

- **SGLang.** I opened [PR #46153](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/46153) to support NVIDIA's Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP4-Local checkpoint on a single Blackwell GPU. It started as a branch I hacked together to run the model at home since there was no upstream support. I'm looking for more: bug fixes, model support, multi-modality knobs, throughput.
- **Open models.** If it fits in one RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, I've probably tried it. Lately that's Qwen3.8-27B and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on SGLang and vLLM, which also power my coding agents through OpenCode.
- **Learning in public.** I keep my repos documented, including the code and write-ups from my AIMO work.
